# Student food and sports research — November 18, 2026

Scope: public, visitor-accessible college/student settings and affordable orders in Providence (Dec 9–11) and Hartford (Dec 11–14). Actual published prices replace generic estimates for the selected orders. Budget envelopes remain spending allocations, not restaurant quotes.

## Chosen Providence stops

- **East Side Pockets, 278 Thayer Street:** Brown Undergraduate Admission calls it a student favorite: https://admission.brown.edu/beyond-the-tour . Restaurant homepage independently identifies Brown/RISD/student customers. Its own Online Order link redirects to https://www.ediningexpress.com/live40/617/1130/ . Browser-rendered menu shows falafel wrap $9.26, chicken/gyro wraps $12.04. A sample cart with one unmodified falafel wrap showed subtotal $9.26, tax $0.74, processing fee $1, total $11 before tip. Sample cart cleared; no purchase, payment, account signup or restaurant contact. Fee is an observed online fee, not an assumed in-person charge. Thursday hours 10am–2am verified on https://www.eastsidepocket.com/hours.html . Planned Thursday lunch.
- **Antonio’s, 256 Thayer:** official-linked live Toast menu https://order.toasttab.com/online/antonios-pizza-by-the-slice-thayer-st lists cheese slice $3.99, pepperoni $4.99. Two cheese slices with assumed ordinary RI 8% prepared-meal tax: $8.62, excluding any online fees/tip. Student connection: Brown Classics student account https://classics.brown.edu/news/2025-06-06/class-2025 . Wednesday-only offers are not applied to Thursday. Alternate lunch, not an extra meal.
- **Ceremony, 406 Brook Street:** direct pickup menu https://order.toasttab.com/online/ceremony lists Americano $4.50, latte $6.25, strawberry matcha $7. Computed with RI 8% meal tax: $4.86/$6.75/$7.56, before optional tips/platform fees. Official location hours https://www.ceremonypvd.com/locations list weekdays 8am–6:30pm. Student evidence: Brown Daily Herald's December 7, 2025 café interviews, https://www.browndailyherald.com/article/2025/12/grounds-for-conversation-students-favorite-coffee-shops-near-campus . Article describes student use and also higher drink costs. Chosen Thursday café before descending to RISD.
